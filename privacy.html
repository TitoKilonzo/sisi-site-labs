What we collect

When you register we ask for your county, ward, full name, and at least one way to reach you (phone or email). You may also choose topics and leave a view on the draft budget. We do not ask for your national ID number or date of birth.

Why we collect it

We use these details only to send you notices, draft bills and budget hearings that affect your ward, and to record the views you choose to share. We collect only what that purpose needs, in line with the data minimisation principle of Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

Practice site notice

This site is a learning exercise. Its registration form sends data to a public test service (httpbin.org), so please enter made-up details only. Anything you submit there leaves this site and cannot be taken back.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by writing to habari@sisi.example.
